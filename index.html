An AI community manager for a coaching business

Scott Northwolf runs Self-Improvement Nation and sells coaching through Skool communities. He needed a team member who could answer posts and DMs the way he does, all day, and book qualified people into sales calls.

  • Scraped and tagged his real replies by intent, tone and sales stage, then fine-tuned a model on them
  • Two-model pipeline: a picker scores posts against the ideal customer, a writer replies in his voice with retrieved examples (RAG)
  • Playwright automation for comments, likes, warm-up and DM follow-ups, with booking-link guards
  • Packaged as a Windows desktop app so the client can start, stop and review every job

Trading systemJun – Nov 202667 + 21 commits

A hedged prediction-market desk for a crypto trader

The client trades daily BTC, ETH and SOL markets on Polymarket and hedges them with Deribit options. Spreadsheets and manual checks couldn't keep up, and trades were sometimes placed outside the agreed rules.

  • Pricing engine: fair value from the Deribit option chain, edge in percentage points, a put or call spread hedge per position
  • Runner that scans every few minutes and enters only inside the 00:00–07:59 UTC window, with position caps and a daily loss stop
  • "Desk" dashboard: live feeds, ladders per asset, positions, rule settings, a tape of every decision and a HALT ALL button
  • Auto-built compliance report that proves the entry cut-off holds and tracks what skipped trades would have done

Data platformMar – Oct 2026102 commits

Market data backend with an AI trade matrix

The same desk needed one backend for every feed: Polymarket markets, Deribit chains, balances and positions. On top of it sits a screen where AI agents give a probability for each market and show their reasoning.

  • API for Polymarket and Deribit data, sub-account positions and account summaries, with rate-limited CSV exports
  • Daily balance tracking in USD with a scheduler and stored performance history

About

Hi, I'm Pedro. I bet on myself.

I was studying AI at Howest University in Belgium, on the usual path: finish the degree, do unpaid internships, and maybe one day earn the right to work remotely from somewhere sunny. I didn't want to wait years for that.

Nobody likes grey skies and cold winters. I wanted to live somewhere warm, around open, happy people, and it mattered to me enough to bet everything on it. Most people stop at wanting it. I didn't.

I left university, got a job in a café in Ghent and saved up. Then I bought a one-way ticket to Bali with one month of savings and no clients. Six days after landing I closed my first €1,000 freelance project, and I've been freelancing full-time from Bali ever since.

Since then I've built an AI agent that runs a coaching business's community, a hedged trading desk with its own pricing engine, and the backends and dashboards around them. Each project needed something I hadn't done before, and I learned it on the job. That's what you get when you hire me: I move fast, I figure things out, and I deliver, because this is how I live.

How I work

From first call to running software

  1. Write the rules down

    We talk it through, and I send a short written brief with the exact logic and limits before any code.

  2. Ship a safe version

    You see a working version early, in test mode, and nothing goes live until you approve it.

  3. Give you the controls

    A dashboard or desktop app with on/off switches, settings and a log of every decision.

  4. Run and improve

    Docker deploys, restart on reboot, regular updates as your rules change.
